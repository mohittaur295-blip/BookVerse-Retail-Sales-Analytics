# BookVerse Retail Business Intelligence & Sales Analytics

BookVerse Retail Business Intelligence & Sales Analytics is a MySQL-based SQL project developed to analyze bookstore sales, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, genre performance, and inventory position.

The project uses three datasets — Books, Customers, and Orders — connected through primary and foreign key relationships. SQL queries are used to perform data validation, data exploration, sales analysis, customer analysis, product analysis, inventory analysis, and business opportunity identification.

## Key Analysis

- Analyzed total orders, quantity sold, and revenue.
- Identified best-selling books, authors, and genres.
- Analyzed monthly sales trends and sales performance.
- Identified repeat and high-value customers.
- Analyzed customer purchasing behaviour across countries and genres.
- Identified low-selling and never-ordered books.
- Analyzed high-priced books with low sales.
- Calculated inventory value by book, genre, and author.
- Identified low-stock and high-demand books requiring replenishment.
- Identified high-stock and low-sales books requiring promotional attention.

## SQL Concepts Used

## Business Outcome

The analysis generates actionable insights for sales growth, customer retention, inventory optimization, product promotion, and demand-based replenishment.
